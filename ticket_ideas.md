# Ticket ideas

Brainstormed ticket types and ticket mechanics. Nothing here is built yet — all
entries are **not implemented**.

- **Heavy Weight.** A ticket that slows the player down massively while carried
  (and is lifted once it is scratched, deposited or lost). Not implemented.
- **Noisy pickup.** A ticket that makes a sound when picked up (drawing guests'
  attention to the player). Not implemented.
- **Tough foil.** A ticket with a lot of scratch health, so it takes far more
  scrubbing to reveal. Not implemented.
- **Treasure Hunt.** A ticket that, when picked up, reveals a location on the map
  for the next piece. Picking that piece up reveals a new one; the player must
  assemble **5 pieces** to finish it. Not implemented.
- **Blackout.** A ticket that makes the game very dark and thus very difficult to
  see while it is held (or carried). Not implemented.
- **Cursed Prize.** A high-value ticket that hums while carried, drawing nearby
  guests toward the player. The tension is getting it back to the stash alive.
  Not implemented.
- **Mimic.** A trap ticket that looks valuable but vanishes the moment it is
  scratched, spawning a guest next to the player (or revealing Empty). Not
  implemented.
- **Bloodletting.** Each scrub also drains a little stamina (on top of foil
  damage), so wanting the prize costs you the energy to escape. Not implemented.
- **The Gambler.** A single-tile ticket with tiny foil health: a visible 50/50
  between nothing and a jackpot. Quick, pure tension. It **must carry a
  drawback** — TBD (not just "you wasted a slot"). Not implemented.
